Load flow in the IVR formulation

The complete problem built by NetworkModel{LoadFlowProblem, IVRFormulation}. It is a system of quadratic equalities: nonconvex, exact, and solved as a feasibility problem.

Variables

symbolkeyindex setbounds
$v^{\text{r}}_{i}, v^{\text{i}}_{i}$:vr, :vi$i \in I$free
$c^{\text{r}}_{a}, c^{\text{i}}_{a}$:cr, :ci$a \in A$free
$c^{\text{sr}}_{e}, c^{\text{si}}_{e}$:csr, :csi$e \in E^{\text{br}}$free
$v^{\text{sr}}_{e}, v^{\text{si}}_{e}$:vsr, :vsi$e \in E^{\text{tf}}$free
$c^{\text{r}}_{u}, c^{\text{i}}_{u}$:cru, :ciu$u \in U$free
$p^{\text{g}}_{u}, q^{\text{g}}_{u}$:pg, :qg$u \in U^{\text{g}}$free

Nothing is bounded. A load flow has a determinate answer, and bounding a variable can only steer the solver away from it.

Objective

\[\min \quad 0\]

Constraints

At the nodes

Reference voltage, $\forall i \in I^{\text{ref}}$:

\[v^{\text{r}}_{i} = v^{\text{m}}_{i} \cos v^{\text{a}}_{i}, \qquad v^{\text{i}}_{i} = v^{\text{m}}_{i} \sin v^{\text{a}}_{i}\]

Voltage magnitude setpoint, $\forall i \in I^{\text{pv}}$:

\[(v^{\text{r}}_{i})^2 + (v^{\text{i}}_{i})^2 = (v^{\text{m}}_{i})^2\]

Current balance, $\forall i \in I$:

\[\sum_{a \in A(i)} c^{\text{r}}_{a} = \sum_{u \in U(i)} c^{\text{r}}_{u}, \qquad \sum_{a \in A(i)} c^{\text{i}}_{a} = \sum_{u \in U(i)} c^{\text{i}}_{u}\]

At the edges

Branch, $\forall e \in E^{\text{br}}$ with arcs $a^{\text{f}}_{e}$ at $i$ and $a^{\text{t}}_{e}$ at $j$:

\[\begin{aligned} c^{\text{r}}_{a^{\text{f}}_{e}} &= g^{\text{sh}}_{e,\text{fr}} v^{\text{r}}_{i} - b^{\text{sh}}_{e,\text{fr}} v^{\text{i}}_{i} + c^{\text{sr}}_{e} \\ c^{\text{i}}_{a^{\text{f}}_{e}} &= g^{\text{sh}}_{e,\text{fr}} v^{\text{i}}_{i} + b^{\text{sh}}_{e,\text{fr}} v^{\text{r}}_{i} + c^{\text{si}}_{e} \\ c^{\text{r}}_{a^{\text{t}}_{e}} &= -c^{\text{sr}}_{e} + g^{\text{sh}}_{e,\text{to}} v^{\text{r}}_{j} - b^{\text{sh}}_{e,\text{to}} v^{\text{i}}_{j} \\ c^{\text{i}}_{a^{\text{t}}_{e}} &= -c^{\text{si}}_{e} + g^{\text{sh}}_{e,\text{to}} v^{\text{i}}_{j} + b^{\text{sh}}_{e,\text{to}} v^{\text{r}}_{j} \\ v^{\text{r}}_{i} - v^{\text{r}}_{j} &= r_{e} c^{\text{sr}}_{e} - x_{e} c^{\text{si}}_{e} \\ v^{\text{i}}_{i} - v^{\text{i}}_{j} &= r_{e} c^{\text{si}}_{e} + x_{e} c^{\text{sr}}_{e} \end{aligned}\]

Transformer, $\forall e \in E^{\text{tf}}$, for every winding $k$ at node $i_k$. A load flow holds the ratio $T_{e,k} = tm_{e,k} \exp(j \, ta_{e,k})$ at its setpoint, so the voltage and the current behind it are expressions, not variables,

\[v^{\text{t}}_{e,k} = \overline{T_{e,k}} \, v_{i_k} / |T_{e,k}|^2, \qquad c^{\text{t}}_{e,k} = \overline{T_{e,k}} \, c_{a_{e,k}} ,\]

and what is left is the star, with $z_{e,k}$ the impedance and $y^{\text{sh}}_{e,k}$ the shunt of winding $k$:

\[v^{\text{t}}_{e,k} - v^{\text{s}}_{e} = z_{e,k} \left( c^{\text{t}}_{e,k} - y^{\text{sh}}_{e,k} \, v^{\text{t}}_{e,k} \right), \qquad \sum_{k} \left( c^{\text{t}}_{e,k} - y^{\text{sh}}_{e,k} \, v^{\text{t}}_{e,k} \right) = y^{\text{m}}_{e} \, v^{\text{s}}_{e}\]

A unit ratio is an alias: $v^{\text{t}}_{e,k} = v_{i_k}$ and $c^{\text{t}}_{e,k} = c_{a_{e,k}}$.

At the units

Generator, $\forall u \in U^{\text{g}}$ at node $i$:

\[p^{\text{g}}_{u} = v^{\text{r}}_{i} c^{\text{r}}_{u} + v^{\text{i}}_{i} c^{\text{i}}_{u}, \qquad q^{\text{g}}_{u} = v^{\text{i}}_{i} c^{\text{r}}_{u} - v^{\text{r}}_{i} c^{\text{i}}_{u}\]

together with the setpoints

\[p^{\text{g}}_{u} = p^{\text{g,set}}_{u} \quad \forall u \text{ at } i \notin I^{\text{ref}}, \qquad q^{\text{g}}_{u} = q^{\text{g,set}}_{u} \quad \forall u \text{ at } i \in I^{\text{pq}}\]

Load, $\forall u \in U^{\text{d}}$, where a FlexibleLoad takes its nominal demand:

\[v^{\text{r}}_{i} c^{\text{r}}_{u} + v^{\text{i}}_{i} c^{\text{i}}_{u} = -p^{\text{d}}_{u}, \qquad v^{\text{i}}_{i} c^{\text{r}}_{u} - v^{\text{r}}_{i} c^{\text{i}}_{u} = -q^{\text{d}}_{u}\]

Storage, $\forall u \in U^{\text{s}}$, holding its setpoint:

\[v^{\text{r}}_{i} c^{\text{r}}_{u} + v^{\text{i}}_{i} c^{\text{i}}_{u} = p^{\text{s,set}}_{u}, \qquad v^{\text{i}}_{i} c^{\text{r}}_{u} - v^{\text{r}}_{i} c^{\text{i}}_{u} = q^{\text{s,set}}_{u}\]

Shunt, $\forall u \in U^{\text{sh}}$:

\[c^{\text{r}}_{u} = -\left(g^{\text{s}}_{u} v^{\text{r}}_{i} - b^{\text{s}}_{u} v^{\text{i}}_{i}\right), \qquad c^{\text{i}}_{u} = -\left(g^{\text{s}}_{u} v^{\text{i}}_{i} + b^{\text{s}}_{u} v^{\text{r}}_{i}\right)\]

Model class and size

Quadratic equality constraints throughout, hence nonconvex; it needs a nonlinear solver. On case14, which has 14 nodes, 20 edges and 17 units:

variablesconstraints
case14192192

Square, as a determinate problem should be.

Validation

Reproduces the AC power flow solution of PowerModels.jl v0.21 on case14 and case5, every voltage magnitude to 1e-6 and every angle to 1e-5 degrees.
