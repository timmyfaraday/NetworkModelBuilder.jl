Load flow in the LPF formulation

The complete problem built by NetworkModel{LoadFlowProblem, LPFFormulation}. Every constraint is affine, so this is a square linear system dressed as an optimization problem.

See The linearized formulation for what the approximations discard.

Variables

symbolkeyindex setbounds
$v^{\text{a}}_{i}$:va$i \in I$free
$p_{a}$:p$a \in A$free
$v^{\text{as}}_{e}$:vas$e \in E^{\text{mw}}$free
$p_{u}$:pu$u \in U$free
$p^{\text{g}}_{u}$:pg$u \in U^{\text{g}}$free

There is no magnitude variable, no current, and no reactive power.

Objective

\[\min \quad 0\]

Constraints

At the nodes

Reference angle, $\forall i \in I^{\text{ref}}$:

\[v^{\text{a}}_{i} = v^{\text{a,set}}_{i}\]

Active power balance, $\forall i \in I$:

\[\sum_{a \in A(i)} p_{a} = \sum_{u \in U(i)} p_{u}\]

There is no magnitude setpoint at a PV node: there is no magnitude.

At the edges

Branch, $\forall e \in E^{\text{br}}$, and two-winding transformer, $\forall e \in E^{\text{tf}}$, differing only in the shift:

\[p_{a^{\text{f}}_{e}} = -b_{e} \left(v^{\text{a}}_{i} - v^{\text{a}}_{j} - ta_{e}\right), \qquad p_{a^{\text{t}}_{e}} = -p_{a^{\text{f}}_{e}}\]

with $b_{e} = -x_{e}/(r_{e}^2 + x_{e}^2)$ and $ta_{e} = 0$ for a branch. The second equation is what makes the model lossless. The ratio magnitude does not appear, so a TapChanger is indistinguishable from a Transformer here.

Multi-winding transformer, $\forall e \in E^{\text{mw}}$:

\[p_{a_{e,k}} = -b_{e,k} \left(v^{\text{a}}_{i_k} - ta_{e,k} - v^{\text{as}}_{e}\right) \quad \forall k, \qquad \sum_{k} p_{a_{e,k}} = 0\]

At the units

\[\begin{aligned} p_{u} &= p^{\text{g}}_{u} &&\forall u \in U^{\text{g}} \\ p_{u} &= -p^{\text{d}}_{u} &&\forall u \in U^{\text{d}} \\ p_{u} &= p^{\text{s,set}}_{u} &&\forall u \in U^{\text{s}} \\ p_{u} &= -g^{\text{s}}_{u} &&\forall u \in U^{\text{sh}} \end{aligned}\]

together with the generator setpoints

\[p^{\text{g}}_{u} = p^{\text{g,set}}_{u} \quad \forall u \text{ at } i \notin I^{\text{ref}}\]

The PV and PQ distinction disappears: with no reactive power and no voltage magnitude, every generator away from the reference node holds its active setpoint and nothing else. A shunt becomes a constant withdrawal of its conductance.

Affine constraints and a constant objective, so any LP solver will do. On case14:

Validation

Reproduces PowerModels.jl v0.21 DCPPowerModel on case14 and case3, every angle to 1e-5 degrees. case5 differs, for the reasons set out in The linearized formulation.
