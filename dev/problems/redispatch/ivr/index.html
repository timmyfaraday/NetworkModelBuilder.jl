Redispatch in the IVR formulation

The complete problem built by NetworkModel{RedispatchProblem, IVRFormulation}. It is a nonconvex quadratically constrained program, exactly as the optimal power flow is: nothing a redispatch adds is nonlinear.

Variables

Everything an optimal power flow has, plus two non-negative volumes per generator and per storage unit.

Each upper bound is clipped at zero, so a market schedule that lies outside the capability can still be brought back into it rather than making the problem infeasible.

Objective

\[\min \quad \sum_{n \in \mathcal{N}} w_{n} \sum_{u \in U^{\text{g}} \cup U^{\text{s}}} \left( c^{\uparrow}_{u} p^{\uparrow}_{u,n} + c^{\downarrow}_{u} p^{\downarrow}_{u,n} \right)\]

Linear in the volumes, whatever the degree of the generation cost polynomial: that polynomial no longer enters the objective, only — through redispatch_price — the prices it stands in for.

Constraints

At the nodes

Identical to the optimal power flow: the reference angle, the voltage magnitude limits, and the current balance.

At the edges

The physics are identical, and so are the angle difference limits and the phase shifter ratio. The rating is the one thing that changes, and only in where it applies, $\forall e \in E^{\text{mon}}$, per terminal $a$ at node $i$:

\[\left((v^{\text{r}}_{i})^2 + (v^{\text{i}}_{i})^2\right) \left((c^{\text{r}}_{a})^2 + (c^{\text{i}}_{a})^2\right) \le (s^{\text{max}}_{e})^2\]

with $E^{\text{mon}} \subseteq E$ the monitored edges, every edge by default.

At the units

The power-against-current relations are those of an optimal power flow, with the split added on top, $\forall u \in U^{\text{g}}$:

\[p^{\text{g}}_{u} = p^{\text{g,mkt}}_{u} + p^{\uparrow}_{u} - p^{\downarrow}_{u}\]

and $\forall u \in U^{\text{s}}$:

\[p^{\text{sd}}_{u} - p^{\text{sc}}_{u} = p^{\text{s}}_{u} + p^{\uparrow}_{u} - p^{\downarrow}_{u}\]

Both are affine, which is why a redispatch is no harder to solve than the optimal power flow underneath it.

Across network indices

The state of charge of a storage unit and the energy of a flexible load, exactly as in an optimal power flow, plus one equality per preventive measure per contingency:

\[x_{c,n} = x_{c,n^{0}} \quad \forall n, \; \forall x \in X^{\text{prev}}_{c}\]

with $n^{0}$ the base case of $n$. Here $X^{\text{prev}}_{c}$ is $(p^{\uparrow}, p^{\downarrow})$ for a generator, $(p^{\text{sc}}, p^{\text{sd}}, p^{\uparrow}, p^{\downarrow})$ for a storage unit, $(t^{\text{r}}, t^{\text{i}})$ for a phase shifter and $(tm)$ for a tap changer — see redispatch_controls.

Model class and size

Nonconvex and quadratically constrained, as the optimal power flow is, with a linear objective in place of a quadratic one. Needs a nonlinear solver.

using NetworkModelBuilder, Ipopt

result = solve_rd("case5.m", IVRFormulation, Ipopt.Optimizer;
                  redispatch = Redispatch(; monitored = [7]))
